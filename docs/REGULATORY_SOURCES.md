## Source review and publication procedure

Every KMS document created by the external-source staging command has `review_status=review_required`. It cannot pass the existing QA or approval operations as-is. Quarantined provisions may have no KMS document at all. There is no archive-promotion endpoint.

1. A reviewer with `knowledge`, `qa`, `compliance` or `admin` access inspects `/api/v1/trust/knowledge/sources/{source_id}` and downloads the original through its `/download` route. Compare the download hash, cited physical PDF pages and extracted wording, particularly OCR defects and section boundaries.
2. Resolve definitions, exceptions, explanatory sections, amendments, court holdings, effective dates and lender/product applicability. Read linked context in the original sources; a `context_refs` entry is a review pointer, not proof that the context was reconciled. Leave unresolved material unpublished.
3. After source review, an authorized `knowledge` or `admin` actor creates a new, corrected draft through the existing `POST /api/v1/trust/knowledge/documents` operation with `review_status=verified`. Supply the HTTPS source URI, a distinct reviewed version, article/page citation and archived PDF SHA-256 in `source_locator`, justified effective interval and intended access roles. Keep the original archive and staged draft unchanged. Do not label material verified merely to unblock QA.
4. An independent `qa` actor uses `POST /api/v1/trust/knowledge/documents/{document_id}/qa`; a separate human acting as `compliance` uses the existing `/approve` operation with a review note. Both approvals bind the exact content hash. Mutation requests retain the existing `Idempotency-Key` requirement. Approved content is still subject to effective-date, access and retirement checks at retrieval.

Preserve Indonesian source language in the original PDFs and evidence excerpts. Any future English explanation or translation is a separately identified, cited derivative with its own source review, QA and human approval; it must not replace or masquerade as the Indonesian legal text. This release does not generate or approve those derivatives. Knowledge publication never edits hard guardrails or grants a dispatch exception.

## Corrections to the shared Gemini research

The shared research is a set of leads, not an authoritative source or an instruction to change guardrails.

| Claim | Finding and project consequence |
| --- | --- |
| POJK 10/2022 and SEOJK 19/2023 are the current LPBBTI base | Outdated. POJK 40/2024 Article 236 repeals POJK 10/2022, with compatible implementing provisions addressed separately in Article 235. SEOJK 19/2025 XXII(1) expressly repeals SEOJK 19/2023. Do not publish the obsolete documents as current rules. |
| All LPBBTI economic benefits become 0.1% per day in 2026 | Incorrect as a universal rule. Current SEOJK 19/2025 XIV uses funding type, amount and tenor: consumer funding is 0.3% per calendar day up to six months and 0.2% above six months; productive funding has different brackets. XIV(5) also caps combined economic benefits and late fees at 100% of the agreed funding value. Read XXI transition rules and the actual agreement. No billing implementation is authorized by ingestion. |
| Every case must freeze at DPD greater than 90 | Not established by the current passages reviewed. SEOJK 19/2025 XVI classifies arrears beyond 90 calendar days as `macet`; this classification alone does not impose a universal outreach freeze. A stricter internal pause may be a product policy, with its own approved basis. |
| Complaint SLA is always 20 business days | Incorrect. POJK 22 Article 74 sets five business days for oral complaints. Article 75 sets ten business days after complete documents for written complaints, a conditional ten-day extension and additional third-party follow-up provisions. Do not represent 20 days as the universal deadline. |
| Collection may use an emergency contact | SEOJK 19/2025 VIII permits location confirmation with documented contact-owner consent, not collection from that contact. Graceline remains stricter: borrower-only verified recipients. |
| Consent allows the AI to bypass collection windows | POJK 22 Article 62(3) contains a consent exception, but the MVP's stricter scheduler does not enable it. Retrieved legal text cannot override hard rules. |
| All data must be retained for five years | Not substantiated as a universal rule by the reviewed sources. SEOJK 19/2025 VII(10) requires retention to account for applicable law, audit and regulatory examination, then effective deletion. A data-category retention schedule and legal basis require human review. |
| AFPI, SLIK and APPK APIs must be connected immediately | A legal reporting obligation does not prove an API contract, credentials or access rights. POJK 8/2026 Articles 2 and 32 include reporting obligations and transition conditions. API availability and applicability remain unverified; the MVP uses synthetic data and simulated channels. |
| SIP/telephony or Android `FLAG_SECURE` are required legal controls | No such universal legal requirement was verified here. SIP/telephony adapters conflict with the repository's simulated-channel-only scope. Device controls are separate security design choices. |

## Provenance and extraction checks

- Each source has its original URL, publisher, version, effective date, scope, legal status, article/section and one-based PDF page range. PDF bytes are pinned with SHA-256. Extracted page arrays have a separate SHA-256 computed from canonical JSON using sorted keys, compact separators and UTF-8 without ASCII escaping.
- Eight downloaded files passed PDF magic, byte-hash, page-count and manifest-schema checks. Non-article selection markers were checked after whitespace normalization. This does not guarantee every extracted character is correct; approval still requires comparison with the cited PDF, particularly exceptions and definitions.
- OJK's direct links for the three amendments returned HTTP 200 rejection HTML in this environment. Those responses were rejected. Verified official BPK mirror PDFs were downloaded instead. The first five downloads have PDF-type evidence from file bytes; their original HTTP headers were not retained and are not invented in the manifest.
- Signed dates in POJK 32/2025, 38/2025, 39/2025 and 8/2026 are omitted or encoded as placeholder glyphs in the PDF text layer. Their signature pages were rendered and visually checked. Local evidence images remain alongside the ignored source artifacts. Visual checks do not validate digital-signature certificate chains.
- [BPK's POJK 8/2026 catalogue](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/352349/peraturan-ojk-no-8-tahun-2026) says 6 December 2026. The downloaded PDF page 18 visibly says **6 July 2026**, and the [OJK publisher page](https://ojk.go.id/id/regulasi/Pages/POJK-8-Tahun-2026-Pelaporan-dan-Permintaan-Data-Transaksi-Pendanaan-oleh-Penyelenggara-LPBBTI.aspx) explicitly confirms 6 July. The manifest uses the primary PDF date and records the discrepancy.
- SEOJK 19/2025 XX.9 literally refers to number 7 although the collection obligation appears in XX.8. The official text is preserved; it is not silently repaired by the importer.
- A subsequent structural review checked all 61 selected passages across both manifests. Two court reasoning selections initially cut across sentences and were corrected against rendered PDFs: decision 151/PUU-XXII/2024 now selects the complete paragraph [3.13.2] from pages 155–158, stopping before [3.14]; decision 284/PUU-XXIII/2025 selects [3.11] through the conclusion on page 42, stopping before the following statutory citation. Operative holdings are separately anchored to `5. AMAR PUTUSAN`. Original PDF and extracted-page hashes are unchanged; reimport creates corrected manifest snapshots and retains existing unapproved records.
- The UU 27/2022 text layer prefixes the Article 29 heading with a stray dot. Its Article 28 selection now has an explicit end marker so it cannot absorb Article 29(1) or cut across Article 29's continuation. This boundary correction does not repair the PDF's other missing words or OCR defects; its extraction-review flag remains set.

## Remaining review boundaries

The [September 2026 POJK 40 amendment consultation](https://ojk.go.id/id/regulasi/otoritas-jasa-keuangan/rancangan-regulasi/Pages/RPOJK-Perubahan-POJK-40-Tahun-2024.aspx) is a draft, not an enacted rule in this corpus. Current AFPI code-of-conduct editions, lender-specific SOPs, product agreements, regulator integration specifications and a complete legal-retention schedule remain outside this verified collection set. A SPayLater source that redirects to a homepage or exposes another product's terms must not be accepted as SPayLater evidence.

This is a dated, targeted collection with explicit amendment relationships. It is not an exhaustive statement of Indonesian law, continuous legal monitoring, or a claim that every provision is production-ready. Knowledge publication, hard-rule changes and live operational integrations remain separate approval decisions.
